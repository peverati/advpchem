Question (2 min)

Which model chemistry should be closest to the exact answer for a given method: def2-SVP, def2-TZVP or def2-QZVP? Which costs most?

QZVP is closest to the CBS limit and costs the most. TZVP is the usual compromise.

Anatomy of a Q-Chem input

$molecule
0 1
Cl     1.86082400    -0.06541100    -0.00006800
H      1.75394100     1.21098100     0.00034100
$end

$rem
   JOBTYPE              SP
   METHOD               HF
   BASIS                def2-SVP
   SCF_CONVERGENCE      8
$end

What each line means

$molecule

  • Line 1: charge and multiplicity (2S+1)
  • Then: element and x, y, z in Å

$rem

  • JOBTYPE SP: single-point energy
  • METHOD: HF or the functional
  • BASIS: the basis set
  • SCF_CONVERGENCE 8: energy change below 10⁻⁸

In this lab, line 2 of every .xyz file is “charge multiplicity”.

Multiplicity basics

Multiplicity Unpaired electrons Example
1 (singlet) 0 HCl, H₂O
2 (doublet) 1 H atom, radicals
3 (triplet) 2 O atom, O₂

Wrong multiplicity: a perfectly converged, perfectly wrong energy.

Reaction energies

From total energies to ΔE

\[ \Delta E=\sum_i \nu_i E_i\qquad \nu_i>0\ \text{products},\ \ \nu_i<0\ \text{reactants} \]

Convert: \(1\ E_\mathrm{h}=627.5095\) kcal/mol. Error: \(\varepsilon=\Delta E_\mathrm{comp}-\Delta E_\mathrm{ref}\) (signed; positive = overestimate).

For (HCl)₂ → 2 HCl:

\[ \Delta E=(+2)\,E(\mathrm{HCl})+(-1)\,E\big((\mathrm{HCl})_2\big) \]

Think–pair–share

Question (2 min)

Why must you keep all digits of the total energies? What happens if you round to 4 decimals?

Total energies are hundreds of \(E_\mathrm{h}\); reaction energies are thousandths. Rounding to 4 decimals can introduce errors of up to 0.03 kcal/mol, and rounding differently on each species is worse.

Experiment 1

Experiment 1: the question

How large does a basis set need to be?

\[ (\mathrm{HCl})_2\to2\,\mathrm{HCl} \]

From the NCCE30 subset. Reference: 2.01 kcal/mol.

The design

  • 4 methods: HF, PBE, B3LYP, ωB97M-V
  • 6 basis sets: def2-SVP, def2-TZVP, def2-QZVP, cc-pVDZ, cc-pVTZ, cc-pVQZ
  • 2 molecules × 4 × 6 = 48 jobs
  • All short, well within the 10-minute limit

What you will see

  1. Error versus ζ for each method
  2. Two families: Ahlrichs def2 and Dunning cc
  3. Timing versus number of basis functions \(K\), and the fitted exponent

Record, for every job: energy, SCF cycles, wall time, \(K\).
